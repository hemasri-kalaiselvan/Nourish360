FEATURED TOPIC

Food habits & liver health

Frequent excess intake of energy-dense foods high in added sugar or saturated fat can make metabolic health harder to manage for some people. Overall diet, portions, activity, and individual health factors all matter.

Fatty liver can have several causes. Its stage and risks need to be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.

Health information notice
